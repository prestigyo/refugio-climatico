Tazacorte, Santa Cruz de Tenerife

In a typical winter, Tazacorte needs the heating on 0 nights out of 151, and AEMET has not recorded a single frost there in 8 measured winters. Its worst winter of the series needed 0.

nights below 10 °C, of 151
150

8

114
tropical nights a year — the price of the mild winter

Every figure is the median of 8 winters from 1 November to 31 March, measured at the AEMET station named Tazacorte, at 62 m. Never a mean: averaging a hard winter with a gentle one invents a mild one that did not happen.

The last column is the part a brochure leaves out. A winter this mild is paid for in summer, and here the bill is 114 nights a year above 20 °C. Why that trade-off is almost impossible to escape: the best climate in Spain, year round.
